Theme Taxonomy (Max 5 Themes)

1. Market-Open Latency & Stability

Includes: Server errors, app crashes, freezes, network timeout toasts, auth logouts during 09:15–10:30 AM.

Excludes: General feature requests or slow onboarding.

2. Withdrawal Latency & Settlement

Includes: T+1/T+2 payout delays, wallet deducted without bank receipt, missing balances, automated bot handling.

Excludes: Third-party payment gateway initial deposit failures.

3. Pricing, MTF & Hidden Fees

Includes: High brokerage complaints, Margin Trading Facility interest ambiguity, auto square-off penalties, DP charges.

Excludes: Exchange-mandated regulatory STT/SEBI turnover fee queries.

4. KYC & Account Setup

Includes: Delays in document review (Aadhaar/PAN), re-KYC freezes, failed bank account penny drops, slow activation.

Excludes: Existing users updating standard app settings.

5. Charting & Execution Tools

Includes: TradingView/ChartIQ discrepancies, candlestick render delays, missing technical indicators, SL-M order restrictions.

Excludes: General server outages covered under Theme 1.

How to Run Weekly

  1. Export the trailing 8–12 weeks of reviews from Google Play Console and App Store Connect as a CSV.
  2. Ensure columns include at minimum: platform, rating, title, text, date.
  3. Open this dashboard on the "Dashboard" tab.
  4. Drag and drop the CSV file into the upload zone (or click the sample test button).
  5. Click Generate Weekly Note. The system will auto-scrub PII and classify themes client-side.
  6. Download the PDF/Doc from the Customer Pulse header, or use Draft Email / Copy Email below.
